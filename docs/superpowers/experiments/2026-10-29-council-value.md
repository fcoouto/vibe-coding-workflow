and functional qualification; this document authorizes no inference or experiment

## Question and proposed case study

Does Council discover material evidence or objections beyond a strong native model
working alone, enough to justify extra time, credits and user attention?

After Council is usable, compare its response to the user's thread-splitting
question with a separate strongest-native-only investigation. Give both the same
question, approved snapshot and shared public references. Declare models, maximum
supported reasoning and time/resource allowances beforehand. Equal effort labels
do not establish equal compute. Native-only context must not inherit Council reports.

Inspect anonymized outputs against sources for supported decisive claims, missed
material objections and actionable conclusions. Record elapsed time, observed
external cost, available native consumption and user effort to interpret results.
Neither self-grading nor majority agreement establishes correctness. One case
cannot support general effectiveness claims.
