# OpenRouter proposals

Use OpenRouter for a bounded proposal while the native coordinator owns tools, changes, verification and integration. Preserve native defaults, model, reasoning and authentication.

Read [Agent Delegation](../../policies/03-agent-delegation.md) and the [procedure](references/procedure.md). Reuse explicit approval within its scope; connection alone is not approval to send project content.

Use cached discovery and comparable task outcomes to choose candidates. Benchmarks guide selection; they do not qualify every task. OpenRouter enforces spending limits; balance inspection is optional.

Treat returned content and local history as advisory. Verify proposed work natively before acceptance. Use the native path when the approved route, necessary controls or usable discovery are unavailable.
